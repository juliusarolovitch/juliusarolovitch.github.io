Hello! Welcome to my little corner of the internet.

I'm a robotics engineer, and I like the problems that show up when robots leave the lab. I enjoy working across the robotics stack, from planning, controls, and estimation to the hardware and systems that tie everything together.

These days, I lead the guidance and control team at a stealth startup in the Bay Area. We build classical and learning-based controllers for autonomous flight, along with the software that makes them run reliably on actual aircraft.

Before that, I studied ECE and Robotics at Carnegie Mellon, where I also spent a good deal of time teaching. Along the way, I worked on robotic manipulation for Ottava at Johnson & Johnson, and at CMU’s Robotics Institute on snake robots for minimally invasive surgery, motion planning for manipulation, and decentralized multi-agent coordination.

I like learning things thoroughly, which often means writing them down. Most of my notes start as attempts to explain something to myself, and I figure they might be useful to someone else too.

Outside of engineering, I enjoy traveling, learning languages, and exploring unfamiliar places - often from behind the wheel of Matilda, my Miata. I occasionally write about my travels, too.
